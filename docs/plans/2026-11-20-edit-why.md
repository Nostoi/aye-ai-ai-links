# Your why in Edit link (#351) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Edit dialog lets the owner change or remove a link's why, saved as theirs, in the same Save and Undo as the dialog's other fields.

**Architecture:** The why joins the owner's edit seam the way intent did (#339): `EditInput.why` in core, `LinkEdit.why` with an expected/set pair in the store contract, written inside `applyEdit`'s one transaction and recorded on the existing `link_edits` why columns, which `undoEdit` already restores. The route parses a `why` and `whySeen` field pair; the dialog sends them only when the box's text changed; cards mark their why element so the dialog's `showCard` redraws it from the answer.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Hono, better-sqlite3, Vitest, happy-dom page tests.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-20-why-and-tags-dialogs-design.md` (mockup `docs/design/why-and-tags-mockup.html`, options E1 and G1; owner rulings on #351).

## Global Constraints

- The owner's why is written with source `stated`. A why the dialog did not change is not sent, so a guess stays a guess.
- Blank text removes the why (the existing `cleanWhy` rule in `src/core/links.ts`).
- Stale check: when the Save changes the why, the store writes only while the link still holds the why the dialog opened with (text and source, or none); otherwise it writes nothing, picture/title/tags included, and core answers `conflict` with `WHY_CONFLICT = "This link's why changed since you opened it. Reload the page and edit again."`. A Save that leaves the why untouched carries no why check and never writes the why, as tags and intent work today (decided after the design challenge; the mockup note is corrected to match).
- Each card keeps its own why wording when redrawn: rows and Ask AI cards use `whyText`, search results keep `whyLine`'s "Why: …", the featured card keeps its "You saved it for" label and "Why (a guess): …" for a guess.
- Field: label "Why you saved it", under Title, above Tags. A guess opens with the label suffix "(a guess)" and the hint "An AI guessed this. Change it to make it yours."; editing the text drops both.
- No migration: `link_edits` already has `why_changed`, `prior_why_text`, `prior_why_source`, `written_why_text`, `written_why_source`, and `restoreCovered` already restores them.
- Controls ≥ 44px; refusals in `#edit-error`; README updated in this PR; ROADMAP decision-log entry for the rulings.

## Review Focus

1. **A why changed by an AI while the dialog is open:** Save is a 409 with `WHY_CONFLICT`, and the title, tags and intent sent with it are not written either.
2. **Undo after removing a why:** the why and its source come back exactly (a stated one stays stated, a guess stays a guess).
3. **A guess saved untouched alongside a title change:** the why stays inferred; the receipt records no why change.
4. **Whitespace-only edit of a stated why:** treated as removal, not as text "  ".
5. **Featured Home card whose why is removed, added, or Undone:** removing it brings back the amber outline and Tell me why at once; adding one hides them; Undo reverses either. The featured card always renders its Tell me why form, hidden while a why exists.
6. **Tell me why, then Edit link without a reload:** the dialog opens on the reason just given, and Save succeeds.
7. **A row whose why is removed:** it shows the summary, as a row with no why does; adding a why hides the summary.

Each line has its test in the owning task below.
