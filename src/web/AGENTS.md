# src/web

Rules for this folder only. The reference is `docs/design/home-calm-desk-mockup.html` (option A) and spec §6. The Ask AI panel's reference is `docs/design/ask-ai-panel-mockup.html`, which governs over the text of its design (#159).

## Design rules (spec §6)

1. **Room to breathe:** big text, generous space, few things per screen.
2. **Size = importance:** the one thing worth attention is the biggest thing.
3. **Color means one thing each** (below).
4. **Pictures are small accents:** your words lead, and a picture is a small fixed square beside them, never a wide crop (ROADMAP 2026-10-20; #194 and #195 bring All links and Home to it). The featured card on Home's square is 168px (#295). The one exception is the Pictures view of All links and space pages, which the owner switches to on purpose and which shows each picture across its card (#191, #194). A page's preview shows unless its site makes pictures of words (`isWordPictureHost`, `src/core/word-picture-host.ts`), which shows the site's icon. A link with no picture shows its initials on `--pic`, never in its space's color. The saved copy is a second exception (owner, 2026-10-21, #223): a picture standing alone in its article runs edge to edge across the sheet on a band of `--bg`, keeps its proportions, is centred, isn't cropped, and is no taller than 60% of the screen. A picture inside text or a cell stays inline.
5. **Ask, don't make you dig:** open on a question; search understands vague memories.
6. **No guilt counters:** no unread totals; small, finishable nudges. The profile's Stats section is the exception: it shows a not-opened count, because the owner only sees it when they go looking (owner, 2026-10-22, #161).

## Color

- Each space (pinned tag) keeps one color everywhere, through the `.space-*` classes. The colors are `SPACE_COLORS` in `src/core/tag-space.ts`. A new pin takes a color no tag holds while one is free; two spaces may share a color past that, or when the owner picks one. A card's space comes from `linkSpace` and a tag's color from `spaceColor` (`src/core/link-space.ts`); both pass only `SPACE_COLORS`, and only for a pinned tag. An unpinned tag keeps its stored color but shows none, except on the Tags page, which edits that color and shows it. Other class names never start with `space-`.
- Amber only ever means "needs you". Never use it for a space. A link that seems gone gets the amber band along its bottom edge, never an amber outline (#298).
- Everything else is neutral. The teal logo is the one brand exception.

## Pages

- Pages make no outside requests: no remote images, scripts, stylesheets or fonts. Link navigation is fine. The fonts are the app's own, served from `assets/fonts/` by `fonts.ts` at a versioned address, and the saved copy's CSP allows them through `font-src 'self'`.
- Every page renders through `page()` and the theme tokens in `theme.ts`; no page brings its own colors.
- The gaps between items in a group (cards in a tray, rows in a list) come from the `--space-*` scale in `theme.ts`. In a group with its own padding, such as a tray or the band, the gaps are never bigger than that padding (#305). Padding inside a control, a label's own margin and values the owner set in a mockup may sit off the scale.
- The top bar sits on the `--tray` band (`.bar-band`, only on pages with the menu). Places come first (Home, All links, Spaces and tags; the current one looks pressed in), then actions as raised white pills or circles (Search, Save, Ask AI), then the account. Save is an action, not a place, so it never joins `PAGE_LINKS`; the phone menu lists it after the places (owner, 2026-11-13, #305). The band is a size container named `bar`: its layouts change with its own width (`@container bar`), never the window's, so an open Ask AI panel or a scrollbar can't wrap it.
- Controls are at least 44px tall. Fields are outlined with `--field`.
- Removing or turning something off shows an Undo bar instead of asking first. The action is done at once, and Undo reverses it with a second request; nothing waits for the bar to close. The bar is `page()`'s `undo` option and its `showUndo`.
- A link card's actions live in one ⋯ menu in a fixed order, mildest first (owner ruling, #284, replacing #192 and #198's): Put it away, Done with it, Keep handy (Not handy anymore once kept), Edit, Remove saved copy, Delete link. Not now, Put it away and Done with it carry a small grey line saying what happens (`NOT_NOW_HINT`, `PUT_AWAY_HINT`, `DONE_HINT`). Only Delete link has the trash can. Cards build it with `editMenu` (`edit-link.ts`); a Handy tile's holds only Done with it and Keep handy, Keep handy already done, and no Put it away. A link done or put away has no first group: its menu starts at Edit, then Bring it back (`doneLinkScript` works all three state rows). Keep the menu to six items: a new one replaces one or argues for its place. The owner chose six, for Put it away (#198), and Home's Also today rows seven (below). Every row is a button; nothing in the menu links away. Rows stay neutral, with a line icon and no color. Every card's ⋯ is flat grey dots with a soft fill on hover or open; only in the Pictures view, where it sits on a picture, does it keep its frosted circle (#295). Every card's ⋯ sits in one place, 4px from the top-right corner of the card (of a search result's body, which on a phone is under the picture), with a 44px tap area; the title and caption area reserves room for it, and no rule moves it per card (#297). Every ⋯ menu (this one and the account menu) carries `name="menu"` so only one is open, and `page()`'s menu script closes it on Escape, an outside press or focus leaving it (#201).
- A card's grey line (`cardLine`, `card-line.ts`) names its space, its site and, where a page shows it, when it was saved, then links **Saved copy** when there is one, then ends with Put away or Done on a link done or put away (`linkRow`); missing parts drop out. It is the one link to a saved copy on All links, space pages and search results (#194); a space page leaves its own space out. The dots between parts are the theme's, drawn after a shown part with a shown part later, so Remove saved copy hides the link with its dot.
- A page that passes `space: null` to `cardLine` gets an empty hidden `.line-space` slot (name in `[data-space-name]`), which the edit dialog's `showCard` fills and shows when a Save gives the link a space (#239); a page that passes no `space` (Home's cards) has none. The name the line shows is `lineSpace` (`src/core/link-space.ts`), the one rule for it, used by the space page's rows and by `cardState` in `http.ts`.
- The edit dialog's tags (#239): the list root that holds the cards carries `data-within` (the space page's list, with `data-own="1"`, and a search within a space), and every Save and Undo sends them so the server answers the card's space as that list reads it. A card's `data-tags` is `editData`'s JSON of its tags in order; the dialog sends the final names with the tags it opened with, and a link whose tags changed meanwhile is a 409 that writes nothing. The dialog reads `/tags/names` once per open, for suggestions only; it still works without them.
- All links and space pages show their links as rows (`linkRow`, `link-rows.ts`) in one white card per month: picture, title, the why when there is one (else the summary; plain text for a stated or asked why, "Why (a guess): …" for an inferred one, #297), the grey line, and the ⋯ menu. The List / Pictures switch changes only `data-view` on the page's `<main>`, so both views share one markup and the theme draws each; the choice is the `aye_view` cookie, per browser, which the server reads (`viewOf`) so the page draws right away.

Handy, Worth a look today and Your spaces each sit on a rounded tray (`.handy`, `.worth`, `.spaces`; not Tidy up's start line): `background:var(--tray)`, the colour the top bar shares, never a percentage of its own. Its icon starts on the cards' top line, so its top padding is the tray's. The icon and the word are `aria-hidden`. The dark values of `--tray` and `--spine` are provisional until the owner has seen them in a dark screenshot, and `--tray` is the top bar's too.

  - The featured card (owner, 2026-11-12, mockup card B) has its picture on the left, level with the title, 168px square (#295), and the ⋯ in its top-right corner, flat and 4px from the edges (#295). Its stated or asked why sits under a small grey "You saved it for" label, without quotation marks; an inferred why stays "Why (a guess): …" with no label (owner, 2026-11-12, #295). It holds Home's one filled button outside the search, Open it, then Not now and Done with it, in that order, as round icon buttons at the right end of the row (`slideButton`, `slide-button.ts`; #298). The widths are measured; their values and how they were measured are in `theme.ts` and the commit that set them. Each opens leftward on hover or keyboard focus to show its label and hint (`NOT_NOW_HINT`, `DONE_HINT`); on a touch screen (`hover:none`) the words always show. A refusal never collapses it.

## Patterns (#311)

The catalogue for a new page. Where a rule already exists above, this names it; only the entries marked *new* are written here. Owner decisions are in `ROADMAP.md`'s dated log (2026-11-13 #305, 2026-11-14 #306) and the issues named.

**Where each pattern is written down**
- Tray and spine: "Home's trays and spines". Top bar band: "The top bar sits on the `--tray` band". Round sliding buttons and the gone band: "The seems-gone band" and Home's featured card (`slideButton`, #298).
- Card with a ring: `--card-ring` after a `--shadow-*` (below). Pictures, initials and the letter square: design rule 4 and the tray's last bullet (#304).
- Text hierarchy: the card's grey line bullet (`cardLine`) and `ROADMAP.md` 2026-11-13 (#305, part 2). Type sizes: "Type steps" (below). Spacing scale: the `--space-*` bullet. Contrast floors: the spine bullet (`theme.test.ts`).

**Buttons** (*new*). `button` is a `--radius-pill` pill on `--surface` with a `--line` border; `button.primary` and `a.button.primary` fill `--ink` and mark a form's or dialog's main action (Find it, Sign in, Save, Allow), one per form; the saved copy's end has none. A round icon button that opens sideways is `slideButton`; the bar's raised pills are `.bar-pill`. States: hover is a tint of `--ink` or the border moving to `--field`; keyboard focus is the focus ring (below); `button:disabled` is dimmed with the default cursor; busy is the page's busy mark (`whileBusy`, `cardBusy`) with its look (`data-busy`, below). Pressed (owner, 2026-11-15, #314): a held-down control's fill is one step darker than its hover tint, as a `color-mix` of `--ink` like the hover tints, and nothing moves (no scale, no shadow). It applies to `button`, `.primary` and `a.button.primary` (also the border), `slideButton`, `.bar-pill`, a Handy tile's actions, ⋯ menu rows and Tidy up's choices, and beats their hover, focus and selected fills (the open Ask AI pill has its own rule in `askCss`). It is instant (`transition:none`) and not drawn on a disabled button. Every page runs one passive, empty `touchstart` listener on `document` (`TOUCH_JS` in `page.ts`), which iOS Safari is reported to need before it applies `:active`; it is on the copy page's CSP list with the page's other scripts, and a real-iPhone check is still open. Busy: `[data-busy]` dims to 60% and shows the progress cursor on it and everything inside it; a `slideButton` marked `data-working` shows its words as if open. Use `data-busy`, not `aria-busy`, which Show older's own state uses.

**Inputs** (*new*). `input` is a pill and `textarea` a rounded rectangle (its corner typed, not a card token), both `--surface` with a `--field` outline, under a `label`, and at least 44px tall. The search box is `.search-box`.

**Alerts** (*new*). A form or action reports its failure in a `role=alert` element, empty until something fails (`[role=alert]:empty` is hidden), filled with plain words. On a card and at the saved copy's end the Undo bar announces successes and the alert only errors (the bullet above). Amber is for "needs you", not for error text.

**Empty states** (*new*). One plain sentence in the page's voice saying what is missing and how it fills ("Nothing opened yet. Links you open from this site show here."), no picture and no counter (design rule 6). A group that can empty by hiding hides through a `:has()` rule, as `WORTH_EMPTY` and `.done-empty` do, not a script.

**Focus ring** (*new*). `:focus-visible` is the one `--blue` ring on everything; a new control adds no outline of its own and removes this one only to draw it elsewhere: a whole-card link draws it on its `::after`, inside the card, with an inner `--surface` ring (`.handy-tile h3 a`, `.home-space-name`); a tray's spine shows it on `:focus` (the spine bullet).

**Layers** (*new*). `z-index` takes a `--layer-*` token, never a number; a new role adds its own token to `shared`, placed against the others. Lowest first: `lift` (an opening slide button, the gone band's pointer, a link above its card's whole-card link), `tile-actions` (a Handy tile's actions and gone band over its whole-tile link), `card-menu`, `open-card` (a card whose ⋯ is open, so its menu covers the next card), `menu` (the account menu), `chrome` (the top bar band and the Ask AI panel) and `undo` (the Undo bar, above the panel, set in `askCss` only). A `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()` is in the top layer and takes no number.

**Shape, colour and motion tokens** (*new*, `shared` in `theme.ts`; each holds the value the stylesheet had when it was added, which `design-tokens.test.ts` proves).
- Corners: `--radius-card` for a content card or tile, `--radius-card-large` for a page's main card and a list's card, `--radius-pill` for a pill control. Every other corner is typed and matches the nearest element of its kind (pictures, menus, alerts, the dialog, the bar's pills, `.worth-row`, `textarea`, Tidy's choice buttons), even where its number equals a token's. A tray's corner is `calc(var(--radius-card) + var(--tray-pad))` written in its own rule, as `--tray-pad` is set on the tray.
- Shadows name depth only: `--shadow-card`, `--shadow-raised`, `--shadow-menu`. A ringed card writes `var(--shadow-card),var(--card-ring)`; a card with no ring (`.note.guess`) leaves it out.
- `--white` is white in both themes (a picture's backing, a chip's text); a page's own white is `--surface`. `--overlay-chip` is the dark layer over a picture. A `::backdrop` takes no `var()`: before Safari 17.4 and Chrome 122 it does not inherit the page's variables, so the edit dialog's backdrop colour is typed (`theme.test.ts` checks).
- Motion: `--motion-pop` (a menu appearing), `--motion-hover` (a fill changing), `--motion-fade` (opacity), `--motion-leave` (a band going), `--motion-slide` (a button opening) and `--motion-label-delay`; easings `--ease-standard` and `--ease-out`. The one `prefers-reduced-motion:reduce` block that ends in `*{…!important}` turns all of it off, so new motion adds nothing there.

**Type steps** (*new*, #312, `shared` in `theme.ts`). Text sizes are eleven ordered steps, `--text-1` smallest to `--text-11` largest, and every `font-size` in `themeCss` is one of them. They are numbered, not named for a role: one role spans several sizes (card titles), so a step has no single meaning, and this is the exception to "a token names a meaning". Pick the step for the text's weight in the page's hierarchy; do not add a step or a typed size for one rule. Roles that use each step today:
- 1: the smallest labels: a slide button's hint, the dialog's picture chip, a keycap, the letter on a space's favicon.
- 2: grey small text: `.small`, hints, "You saved it for", the spine's word, the dialog's page title.
- 3: secondary text and menu rows: tag counts, note meta, the gone band's words, dialog field labels, Tidy up's choice lines.
- 4: controls and everyday lines: buttons, `label`, the top bar's menu, `.lead`, a row's why and summary, links beside a title, the Undo bar.
- 5: a small card's title: a Handy tile, an Also today row.
- 6: the page text (`body`), a list row's title, a small heading inside a card, Tidy up's why and its choice titles, a note.
- 7: a result's or Save link's title; a heading inside a card (a tag, a suggestion, the Stats).
- 8: a panel's, dialog's or month's heading; the phone's featured card title.
- 9: a page's section heading (`h2`), the featured card's title, Tidy up's title and its head.
- 10: a page title on a phone, Tidy up's done heading.
- 11: a page title (`h1`).
Typed on purpose, so changing a step can't move them (`design-tokens.test.ts` lists them): Home's question, "Also today" and the card's 13.5px grey detail line (the three rules `.card-line`, `.card-site`, `.also-why`), the saved copy's reading sizes, the initials' figures, and every `.ask-*` rule. Text that sets no size inherits one from its parent, so a new rule that resets `font` to `inherit` follows its container's step.

**Names for new work** (*new*; no existing class is renamed). A picture box is `-pic` (`feature-pic`, `row-pic`). The grey text roles `--about`, `--soft` and `--detail` stay distinct. Grey secondary text takes `.small`, or `.card-line` inside a card, and a new class is named for what the text is (`copy-notice`), never its colour.

**Ask AI** (*new*). Its `.ask-*` families repeat the button, card and label ideas above. When Ask AI is next touched, reuse the shared families instead of writing more `.ask-*` copies.

**A new page, in order** (*new*)
1. Render through `page()`; colours, type (`--text-N`), `--space-*`, corners, shadows and motion use a token where one fits; anything else is typed to match the nearest existing element of its kind. A `z-index` always takes a `--layer-*` token (Layers, above; `theme.test.ts` fails a number).
2. A card is `--surface`, a corner token and a shadow token with the ring. A group's gaps stay within its padding.
3. Controls are at least 44px tall; a form's main action is its one `.primary`; hover, focus and disabled are the shared ones. A new action that posts passes `look` to `whileBusy` to get the busy look, and calls `answered()` when its answer arrives.
4. Give it its empty state and its `role=alert`.
5. Look at it in light and dark, at 390px and 1280px, and with `hover:none`; check the contrast floors.
6. A route joins the route guard ("New routes"); a user-visible change updates `README.md`.

## New routes

A new web route must pass the route guard in `src/mcp/isolation.test.ts` ("owner isolation coverage"). Every signed-in read path needs a `READ_ROUTES` entry: a sample request with A's marker (A gets 200 with it; a second owner gets no A marker and, for bytes, not A's body), or a `noOwnerData` reason. A path that takes a code in place of a session, and so shows the code's owner's data to anyone holding it (the open link, `/o/:code`), gets a `bearer` reason instead, plus its own test that the code reaches only its own link, the same for every visitor. Every write path needs a `WRITE_ROUTES_TESTED` entry naming its two-owner test, and a signed-in POST joins `CHECKED_PAGES` in `src/app.ts`. The guard also fails any HTML page A receives that makes an outside request.
