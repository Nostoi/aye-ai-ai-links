Your why in Edit link, tags in Save

Two small additions to the dialogs you already use. #351 lets you change or remove a link's why in Edit link…. #352 lets you add tags while you save. Each card changes one thing from the one before it. Faded parts are unchanged from today.

Edit link: where the why goes (#351)

Today the dialog is Title, Tags, What is it for?, Picture. The why is the part you'd most often fix, so both options put it near the top.

E1 · Under the title Recommended

Title, then Why you saved it, then Tags. The two things that are your words sit together.

E2 · Under the tags

Same field, moved below Tags. Keeps today's top of the dialog as it is.

Edit link: a why an AI guessed

Most whys are yours. When an AI guessed one, the dialog has to show it's a guess, and say what Save does with it.

G1 · The guess in the box Recommended

The guess is filled in, and the label says (a guess). Change the words and it becomes yours. Leave it alone and it stays a guess, the way the To keep guess works today.

G2 · The guess beside an empty box

The box starts empty. The guess shows under it with Use it, like the page's own title under Title. An empty box leaves the guess alone.

Removing a why

Empty the box and press Save. The why is gone, and the Undo bar can bring it back. The same bar covers everything else Save changed.

Saved changes to “The quiet cost of meetings”. Undo

Save: where tags go (#352)

The same tag picker as Edit link: your tags are suggested as you type, Enter adds one, x removes it. Tags added here are yours, not guesses.

S1 · Under the why Recommended

Address, Title, Why, then Tags. The order goes from what you must give to what's nice to have.

S2 · Above the why

Same field, moved above Why, next to the title.

S3 · Behind “Add tags”

The dialog stays three fields long. Add tags under Why opens the field when you want it.

S1 while typing

Typing shows matching tags you already use. Enter or a click adds one.

Save: a page you saved before

Your answer: the tags are added to the link you already have, and the dialog says so.

Already saved, tags added

Tags the link already had stay. Only new ones are added, and the note names them.

What stays the same either way

  • Your why and tags in these dialogs are recorded as yours. An AI's later guess can't replace them (#342).
  • Edit link's Save sends only what you changed. If an AI changed the why or the tags you're changing after you opened the dialog, Save is refused with nothing saved, and you reload, as it already works for tags. A why or tags you didn't touch are never written.
  • After Save, every card for the link shows the new why and its space and color at once: Home, All links, your spaces and search.
  • Controls stay at least 44px tall, and errors show in the dialog's message box in plain words, never in amber.
