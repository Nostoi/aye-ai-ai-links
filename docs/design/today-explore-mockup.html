Recommendations

  1. Name: Explore, the owner's choice. One word for the tray; the cards no longer rotate daily, so “Today” would over-promise.
  2. Featured card (still open): if the owner wants round slide icons, 2e: Keep as an always-open labelled pill, Skip and Archive as slide icons, with the check-in-circle icon for Keep. Desktop: the one decision the design wants made is readable without hover, and the other two stay quiet. Phone: no hover, so every icon shows its words anyway and 2e wraps like 2d.
