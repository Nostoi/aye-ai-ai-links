Take two — built from ADHD-friendly design rules

Before the screens, here are the rules I used. Tell me if any of these are wrong for you — they matter more than any one layout.

1 · Room to breatheLots of empty space, big text, few things per screen. Space is what lets your eye rest and land.
2 · Size = importanceThe one thing worth your attention is the biggest thing. Everything else gets visibly smaller and quieter.
3 · Color means one thing eachEach space (pinned tag/project) owns a color everywhere. Amber only ever means "this needs you." Everything else stays neutral.
4 · Pictures to jog memoryReal page images, big. You often remember "the one with the red chart," not the title.
5 · Ask, don't make you digThe page opens on a question in plain words. Search understands vague memories.
6 · No guilt countersNo "214 unread." Small, finishable nudges: one link, three buttons, done.
